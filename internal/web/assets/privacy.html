Privacy Policy

Coyote video chat, operated by SwiftIRC · Effective 10 October 2026

In short
  • There are no accounts, and we don't record calls.
  • Chat history lives only in server memory and disappears when the room closes.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, and we never sell data.

Who we are

Coyote is the video-chat service of the SwiftIRC network, available at vc.swiftirc.net and through the Coyote apps for iOS and Android. SwiftIRC operates the service and is responsible for the data described here. Contact us at support@swiftirc.net.

What we process, and why

  • Your display name. You choose a name when you join, or it comes from the IRC nick carried by an invite link. Everyone else in the room sees it. We use it only to show who is in the call.
  • Invite links and tokens. If you join from an IRC invite, the server checks the link to confirm your nick and channel role. Invite links expire, and the apps never save them.
  • Audio and video. Your microphone and camera stream pass through our server to the other people in the room in real time. We do not record, store or analyse calls.
  • Chat and polls. The last 200 chat messages in a room are kept in server memory so people who join late can catch up. Polls and the list of who is in the room are kept the same way. All of it is gone when the room closes or the server restarts. We keep no database.
  • Connection details. While you are connected, the server holds your IP address in memory so it can route your call and enforce room bans set by channel operators. Coyote does not write names, messages or IP addresses to its logs. Like most websites, the web server in front of Coyote may keep standard access logs (IP address, time and page requested) for security and troubleshooting.
  • Your avatar (optional). If you enter an email address for a Gravatar picture, your device turns it into a one-way hash, and only that hash is sent. Your email address never leaves your device. Avatar images are then loaded from gravatar.com, which handles that request under Gravatar's own privacy policy.

What stays on your device

Your browser or the app remembers your display name, the email for your avatar, your microphone and camera preferences and, in the apps, a list of recently joined rooms. This is stored only on your device. You can clear it from your browser settings, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Permissions the apps use

  • Microphone and camera: only during a call, so others can hear and see you.
  • Bluetooth: to route call audio to your headset.
  • Notifications (Android): to show the ongoing-call notification that keeps your call running in the background.

The apps show calls in your phone's call screen (CallKit on iPhone, the call service on Android) so a call keeps working when the screen is locked. Coyote calls are not added to your phone's call history.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or anyone else. What you say and show in a room is shared with the other people in that room, which is the point of a call. Bear in mind that other participants could record a call on their own devices.

Children

Coyote is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has used Coyote in a way that concerns you, contact us and we'll help.

Your choices and rights

Because we keep no accounts and no stored copies of calls or chat, there is usually nothing for us to look up, export or delete once a room has closed. You can still ask us any question about your data, or exercise any rights you have under the law where you live, by emailing support@swiftirc.net.

Changes

If we change how Coyote handles data, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be announced on the SwiftIRC network.
